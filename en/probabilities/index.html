Summon rates

Rate table gacha_v2 · content version 1.3.4 · in effect since 2026-09-25

Evo Legion has one randomized item: the species summon. This page is generated from the same game data our server draws from, and matches Growth → Odds & guarantees in the game.

  • Price: 1 draw for 300 Nebulite, 10 draws for 2,700 Nebulite. Nebulite can be bought or earned by playing (first stage clears, missions, the Season Pass and more).
  • Free draw: one a day, resetting at 05:00 Korea time (20:00 UTC).
  • Every way to draw uses the same table, free or paid.
  • A 10-draw is exactly ten single draws in a row. It carries no extra guarantee.
  • Draws happen only on our server, which records the result, the table version and your pity counts.

3. How the species is chosen

Once the grade is set, one species is picked from that grade’s pool in proportion to its weight: a species’ chance is its weight divided by the pool’s total weight.

  • Common: the pool is the species you own. If you own your Wish species, it has weight 3; every other species has 1.
  • Rare fragments, Epic and Legendary: on the standard banner every species in the pool has weight 1. On a pickup banner the featured species has weight 2 if it’s in the pool; the rest have 1.
  • Graft blueprints: all 24 have the same chance (1/24 each).
  • Example: with 7 species you don’t own, one of them featured, a Legendary is the featured species 2/(2+6) = 25% of the time, and each of the others 1/8 = 12.5%.

4. Pity (guarantees)

  • Legendary guarantee: after 59 draws in a row without a Legendary, draw 60 is a Legendary. Getting a Legendary in any way resets this count to 0.
  • Wish guarantee: every 90th draw is a Legendary of the species you picked as your Wish (unlocked plus 20 fragments if you don’t own it, 20 fragments if you do). You can change your Wish at any time on the Growth → Summon screen.
  • Other Legendaries — lucky draws and the Legendary guarantee — don’t reset the Wish count. When the Wish guarantee fires, both counts go back to 0.
  • If both guarantees land on the same draw, the Wish guarantee applies.
  • Both counts are shared by the standard and pickup banners and by free and paid draws, and carry over when banners change.
  • The draws remaining are always shown on the bars in the Growth screen.

For reference: with the guarantees, the long-run share of Legendary draws is 3.61% (one in 27.7 on average). This is calculated from the rules above and does not change the listed odds.

8. Ways to grow without chance

  • Research: every species beyond the three starters can be unlocked for certain with Nebulite once you clear the stage listed above.
  • Fragment exchange: 300 Nebulite buys 5 fragments of a species you own, up to 3 times a week (resets Monday 05:00 Korea time).
  • Fragments for first clears and your first win of the day, missions, and Season Pass rewards are fixed, not random.
