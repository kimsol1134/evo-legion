Evo Legion

You don’t defend the planet. The legion you evolve does.

Evo Legion is a space roguelite where your swarm fights on its own and you reshape it one card at a time. The battle keeps moving; whenever it pauses, you pick one of three cards. Pick by pick, the legion that meets the boss looks nothing like the one that set out.

  • Three-minute battles you win by choosing, not tapping — no joystick, no skill buttons
  • Ten species, Bonds when two meet, and Apex evolutions when three come together
  • Seven invaders, each with its own arsenal, across 60 stages
  • A new Daily Trial every day, and the Abyss, where deeper floors pay more
  • No stamina. Every summon rate and pity rule is published
